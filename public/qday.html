Agents pay per request.
The treasury is paid in the same transaction.

AutoTasker Hub exposes data APIs behind an x402 paywall: an agent calls, gets a 402 with a price, signs a stablecoin authorisation, and receives data. On the settlement side a commission router splits the payment on chain — 90% to the house wallet, and that house wallet is the treasury. No sweep, no intermediate address, no retired ledger.

One request, end to end

The paid path is the production path: the same price table drives the live API and the on-chain router.

01 · CALLAgent requests /api/x402/hotlist with no account and no API key.
02 · CHALLENGEServer answers 402 + PAYMENT-REQUIRED with amount, asset and payTo.
03 · SIGNAgent signs an EIP-3009 authorisation; the facilitator settles USDC.
04 · FULFILRouter marks the request fulfilled and pays the treasury its 90% share.
05 · DATATrending Chinese social data returns with a settlement receipt in the headers.
